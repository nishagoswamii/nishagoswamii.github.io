Hey, you found this page! 👋

That means you were curious enough to look and I appreciate that more than you know.

Right now, this space is a little quiet. But quiet isn’t empty. Behind the scenes, I’m living stories I haven’t yet found the right words for. I’m learning things I can’t wait to share. I’m building, failing, figuring it out and taking notes along the way.

What’s coming

Some posts I’m currently thinking about:

  • The things I wish someone had told me : little lessons I had to learn the hard way!
  • What it actually feels like : to build something from scratch, with no roadmap and a lot of hope (quick peek I’m working on a IoT project)!
  • Books, ideas, and rabbit holes : the things I’ve been reading and thinking about lately!
  • Honest reflections on the in-between moments : not the highlights, but the quiet middle parts!

Why I write

Writing, for me, is how I understand myself. I write to untangle thoughts that feel too messy to carry in my head. I write to remember. I write because someday, someone else might read these words and feel a little less alone in whatever they’re going through.

That’s enough reason for me!
